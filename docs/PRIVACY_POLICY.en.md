# TriColumns Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 15, 2026

TriColumns (the “App”) is a three-column web browser for macOS provided by Ryo Fujita (the “Developer”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## 1. Information Collected by the Developer

The App contains no analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies used by the Developer to collect usage data, browsing content, account information, or personal information. The App does not send information to a server operated by the Developer.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the URL configured for each column in macOS `UserDefaults`. WebKit may also store website data, including cookies, caches, and local storage, inside the App's sandbox container so that websites work correctly and remain signed in. This information is not transmitted to the Developer.

Downloaded files are stored only in locations you select using the macOS save panel.

## 3. Communications with Websites

The App makes ordinary HTTPS or HTTP connections to websites that you configure or navigate to. By default, the second and third columns display pages on X's website (`x.com`).

Websites displayed in the App may collect cookies, IP addresses, account information, usage data, and other information under their own privacy policies. Data handling by third-party websites is outside the scope of this policy. The App is not affiliated with or endorsed by X Corp.

## 4. Camera, Microphone, and Files

The App permits camera or microphone access only when a website requests it and you approve the corresponding macOS permission prompt. Web-content capture requests are currently limited to secure X/Twitter domains. Captured video or audio is provided directly to the requesting website; neither the App nor the Developer separately stores or collects it.

For uploads and downloads, the App accesses only files or destinations that you explicitly select using macOS file panels.

## 5. Disclosure and Sale of Information

Because the Developer does not collect personal information through the App, the Developer does not disclose, sell, or share personal information for advertising purposes.

## 6. Deleting Information

You can change or clear column URLs in the App's Settings window. To remove all local data, including cookies and caches, quit the App and remove its macOS container (bundle identifier: `st.rio.tricolumns`). Doing so signs you out of websites and removes saved settings.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated policy and effective date will be published on the App's distribution page or repository.
